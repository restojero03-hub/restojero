## The challenge

I needed a professional funnel that construction professionals would trust, and one that could sell a course without me chasing every buyer by hand. That meant solving four things:

- Turning paid Meta Ads traffic into qualified course leads
- Handling offline bank transfer payments without manual follow-ups
- Making sure opt-ins, checkout users and paid students got the right instructions automatically
- Building trust with a professional audience

## The automated follow-ups

Each workflow runs on what the person actually did, so leads and buyers only get messages that apply to them.

### Opt-in email sequence

- Immediate confirmation email
- Course overview and value reinforcement
- Call to action to proceed to checkout

### Checkout follow-up for offline payment

- Automated reminders for people who chose bank transfer
- Step-by-step payment instructions
- Follow-ups to reduce drop-off

### Payment submitted workflow

- Confirmation email
- Next-step instructions
- Onboarding and access guidance

## Tools

- **Ads:** Meta Ads Manager
- **Funnel:** WordPress with FunnelKit Funnel Builder
- **Email automation and CRM:** FunnelKit
- **Tracking:** Meta Pixel, Google Analytics and FunnelKit Analytics
- **Design:** Canva
- **Integration:** Google API, connecting FunnelKit to Google Sheets

## The result

A fully automated lead-to-enrollment system, where paid traffic, email automation and offline payments work together, with no manual follow-ups needed for offline payments.

You can see the first step of the funnel on the [live registration page](https://go.conexapro.io/op/live-ai-driven-construction-project-management-registration/).
