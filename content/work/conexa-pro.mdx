## The problem

Construction companies run projects on data scattered across spreadsheets, chat apps, paper forms and cloud drives. Nobody has one current view of cost, schedule and what actually happened on site.

## What I built

CoNexa Pro brings that work into one platform. Each company gets its own workspace, with unlimited projects, and every project has:

- Estimating and Bill of Quantities, covering material, labor and equipment
- Gantt scheduling with dependencies
- Procurement and inventory
- Daily site reports with weather, hours and work done
- Cost tracking, with budget vs actual
- An AI assistant

## The SaaS foundation

This is the part most MVPs skip, and it's what makes CoNexa Pro a real product you can sell.

- **Tenancy:** each company has its own workspace, with unlimited projects per company.
- **Access:** 6 roles plus project-level access, with permission checks on every API call.
- **Plans:** Starter, Business and Enterprise, with limits on users, AI credits and storage.
- **Enrollment:** public, private-link and internal plans, with enrollment rules enforced on the server.
- **Billing:** subscription billing via Xendit.
- **Security:** Better Auth sessions, and Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection on sign-up.
- **Files and email:** files on Cloudflare R2, email via Resend.

## The AI layer

The assistant summarizes project status, drafts daily, weekly and monthly reports, answers project questions, and analyzes schedule and progress. It only sees what the person asking is allowed to see, because every request takes the same path:

## How releases work

Changes move through feature branches, then staging, then production. Database changes go through database branching and migration testing first, so a release doesn't put live customer data at risk.
